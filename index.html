About

I am a senior research fellow at the Institute of Economic Sciences and IZA research fellow. My research focuses on human capital, labour economics, and health economics, and I have extensive experience consulting for international organizations such as the World Bank, UNDP, and UNICEF. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Bocconi University in Milan, Italy.

My publications are indexed on Google Scholar.
